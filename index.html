TRY IT TOGETHER

Go on a creation scavenger hunt

Grab your Bible, a pencil, and your curiosity!

  1. Read Genesis 1:1–5 together in your own Bible.
  2. Look around and find three things in creation that make you curious.
  3. Draw one of them. Ask: What do you notice? What would you like to ask God about it?
  4. Take turns thanking God for something you found.

This free activity is a taste of exploring Scripture together, not a page from the purchased study.
